# 0.9.22 release authorization and coverage

The user authorized the release on 2026-10-20 ("Create a new release"), after the
pull requests for it were reviewed, their review threads answered and merged.
Accepted main base: `f5cb4ac780282fe6e22e1ded10f556bf3fbd71c5` (squash merges of
#602, #603, #604 and #605, after the 0.9.21 metadata, acceptance and a
download-count update).
Accepted live runtime: `0bd84426`, which is the code of #602, #603 and #604 at
their final review fixes plus the Debug probe routes; #605 changes only the
download-count workflow.

- No open issue tracks these fixes; none is closed by this release.
- Two-bone IK, live on paused copies with and without a Customize+ profile that
  passes a non-uniform scale down the arm: with End rotation off, drags and a
  30° swivel leave the hand's rotation unchanged; with it on, the hand turns
  exactly with the forearm; switching it either way, enabling, and switching the
  target between Actor, World and Bone move nothing (0.00 cm); after switching, a
  held hand still turns with the followed bone (30° for 30°); a 6 cm drag moves
  the hand 5.92–6.08 cm in every target mode under the profile (7.80 cm before in
  Bone and World) and exactly 6 cm without it; the first gizmo drag on a fresh
  chain no longer turns the hand (20.84° before).
- Not tested: a Customize+ profile that rotates (rather than scales) the hand or
  forearm can still make switching End rotation jump by that rotation; the Entity
  target mode (same code path as Bone); disabling IK is unchanged (the hand keeps
  its IK offset while the arm returns).
- Eyelids, live: two save and load cycles keep every lid bone within 0.14° of the
  original (each cycle closed the lid about 9.6° further before).
- Camera undo, live on the main camera and a free camera: one step after 5 s at
  rest, moves under 5 s apart make one step, a move followed by a bone edit within
  5 s makes two steps that undo in order, undo and redo restore the exact
  framing, and a camera panel edit records only its own step. Not tested in game:
  a camera slider held still mid-drag and a camera restored by lifecycle undo.
- Download count: approving the bot PR's waiting run let auto-merge merge #601;
  the workflow change itself is first exercised by the next scheduled run.

Build, tests, archive verification, vulnerability audit and SBOM are performed
against the exact release head. Release validation does not deploy Debug.
