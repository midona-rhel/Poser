# Environment

The environment is everything about the area around your scene that isn't a thing you can select: the time of day, the weather, the sky, the lighting, fog, rain and wind. Press **Environment** at the top of the main window to open it. It has four tabs in the middle of the window: **Lighting**, **Sky**, **Atmosphere** and **World**.

Most sections start with a **Natural** switch. While it's on, the game runs that part of the environment as usual. As soon as you change a value in the section, Poser takes it over and holds your value, and turning **Natural** back on hands it back to the game.

## Lighting

**Time** sets the time of day and the day of the month. Moving either one stops the clock, and **Freeze time** keeps it stopped. **Restore on exit** gives the clock back to the game when you leave GPose.

**Weather** picks a weather for the area. The list normally shows the weathers that belong to the area, and **All weathers** shows every weather in the game, although the game can refuse one that doesn't belong there. **Transition** sets how many seconds the change takes. **Hold weather** stops the game from changing the weather again, and **Restore on exit** gives the weather back to the game when you leave GPose.

**Lighting** changes the light the whole area is lit with. **Colours** sets the colour of the sun, the moon and the ambient light. **Saturation** and **Temperature** make the ambient light more or less colourful, and warmer or colder. **Light distance** is how far the area's lighting reaches.

**Interior**, in the same section, sets the brightness inside a housing interior, and only works there. It never changes the lighting level saved for the house. **Reset** goes back to the house's own level, and **Release** to the brightness from before you changed it.

## Sky

**Sky** picks the sky texture the area draws, and **Sun visibility** sets how much of the sun shows through it.

**Clouds** picks the textures for the clouds overhead and on the horizon, and their colours. **Shadow stop** and **Cloud height** change how the clouds are shaded and how tall the band on the horizon is.

**Stars** sets how many stars and constellations the night sky has and how bright they are, how bright the band of the galaxy shows, and the colour and brightness of the moon.

## Atmosphere

**Fog** sets the fog's colour and how strongly it applies, how far away it starts, how thick it is, how much it hides, and how it blends into the sky.

**Rain** sets how hard it rains, how thick and what colour the rain is, how fast it falls and spreads, and how many drops splash on surfaces.

**Particles** controls what floats in the air, such as snow or ash: how much of it there is, how big it is, how much it glows, its colour, and how it falls, spreads and spins. **Texture** picks what kind of particle it is.

**Wind** sets which way the wind blows, how far it tilts, and how strong it is.

## World

**Rendering** has **Freeze water**, which stops every water surface in the area, and **Restore water**, which unfreezes it when you leave GPose. **Release all** hands every section Poser has taken over back to the game at once.

**Festivals** shows the area as it looks during a seasonal festival. Press **Add festival** to pick one, choose which stage of it the area shows, and **Remove** a festival to take it away again. **Reset** puts the area's own festivals back.

## Saving the environment

**Save to library**, on the World tab, saves the whole environment so you can apply it again later from the Library. A saved scene also keeps the time of day and the weather, and loading it puts both back.
