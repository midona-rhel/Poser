# Overlays

Overlays are things drawn on top of the game view rather than placed in the world: copies of the game's own dialogue boxes, chat bubbles and status lines for your screenshots, and reference pictures to pose against. They're on the **Overlays** tab of the spawn menu, which you open with **+** in the sidebar. The IK colliders on the same tab are described in [IK](ik.md).

## Dialogue boxes, chat bubbles and status lines

- **Dialogue box** draws the game's NPC dialogue panel. **Speaker** is the name on the plate above the panel, and **Line** is what they say. **Panel** picks the style of the panel, such as a normal one, a thought, an echo or a shout, and **Advance mark** picks the little mark in its corner.
- **Chat bubble** draws the game's speech bubble with one line of text. **Channel** gives it the colours of a chat channel, such as say, party or free company, and **Tint** changes its colour further. **Tail** and **Tail position** set where the point that shows who's speaking sits along its bottom edge.
- **Status line** draws one line of the status bar, with an icon and an effect name. **Effect** is the name, **Icon** picks any status icon from the game, and **Reads as** decides whether it shows as an effect being gained or wearing off.

Select an overlay in the sidebar to edit it on its **Overlay** tab in the middle of the main window. **Visible** hides it, **Scale** makes it bigger or smaller, **Opacity** fades it, and **Text size** sets the size of its words.

To move an overlay, turn on **Drag on screen** and drag it where you want it, or type its **Position** in screen pixels in the inspector. **Centre** moves it to the middle of the screen, and **Reset size** puts its size and opacity back.

**Save to library** keeps an overlay so you can add it again later. **Duplicate** makes a copy, and **Delete** takes it off the screen.

## Reference images

**Reference image** pins a picture of your choice over the game view, for example a pose you want to copy. It stays in the same place on screen while you work, also after you leave GPose and come back. To rename one, right-click it in the sidebar and choose **Rename**.
